04 · Who you need

Four kinds of AI leader. Which ones do you need?

Most companies pass through all four stages, and the person who’s right for the first is rarely the one who can own it outright. So we think about your AI leadership as a sequence, not a single search.

Deep technical expertise is the prerequisite at every stage. It’s rarely the only one.

Book thirty minutes

Not sure which stage you’re at? Working that out is usually the most useful thirty minutes of the whole process.

01 · The Decider

Someone senior enough to say no

When a few experiments are running, and nobody really owns them.

Focus the work on the few ideas with a business owner and a number attached, stop the rest, and set a baseline you can measure against.

02 · The Operator

Someone who gets things running

When the demos impress, but nothing is actually live.

Take one job people do every day and get it fully live, with a name on it, a way to switch it off, and a cost agreed up front.

03 · The Scaler

Someone who can scale it across teams

When one thing works, and now every department wants it.

Turn that success into something other teams can reuse and run themselves. Half the job is technical. The other half is getting people to change how they work.

04 · The Owner

Someone who can own it outright

When the business would notice right away if AI stopped.

Own AI the way a CFO owns money: know what’s running, what it’s exposed to, and have the authority to switch something off.
